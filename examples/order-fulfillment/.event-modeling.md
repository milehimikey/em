## Decisions log

- 2026-08-22: Payment capture is an Automation (`Payment Gateway` processor), not a second
  customer-facing command — the customer's part ends at checkout; nothing about capturing
  the payment needs a person to act. — by Sample Author
- 2026-08-22: Kept `Order Placed.orderId`/`placedAt` and `Payment Captured.orderId`/
  `capturedAt` as system-generated fields the triggering command never supplies, rather than
  padding the commands with fields the client can't legitimately know. `em validate` warns
  on all four — left in on purpose as the documented "this warning is correct and permanent"
  case (see docs/validation.md's Fields Completeness section). — by Sample Author
- 2026-08-22: Gave `Submit Payment`/`Payment Requested` matching fields (`orderId`,
  `amount`) — unlike the two decisions above, this event carries no system-generated data,
  so there was no reason to leave a gap. This is also what makes **Checkout** the one slice
  in this model that actually passes `--slice-ready` cleanly; `browse-catalog` reaches the
  same doc-level readiness but is still blocked by the permanent warnings above. — by Sample
  Author
- 2026-08-22: Considered giving `Open Orders` a `status` field sourced from a
  fulfillment-state event, decided against inventing that event just to satisfy the field —
  left the gap as a genuinely open question on the **View Open Orders** slice doc instead of
  quietly fabricating a source. — by Sample Author
- 2026-08-22: `Manager Review`'s `Pending Payments` view reads directly from `Payment
  Requested`, not from the **Capture Payment** slice's output — nearly modeled the wrong
  arrow here (a manager reviewing *already-captured* payments would defeat the point of a
  pre-capture review screen). — by Sample Author
